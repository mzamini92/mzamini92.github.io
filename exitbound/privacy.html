Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026. This policy describes ExitBound for iPhone and this support website.

ExitBound does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay history, camera feed or saved progress.

Data stored on your device

The app saves puzzle progress, moves, scores, chapter access, preferences, life-recharge deadlines, hint and move balances, and purchase transaction records locally. These records let the app resume play, apply purchases and avoid duplicate credit delivery. They remain until the app updates or replaces them, or you delete the app’s data. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases through Apple

Apple processes in-app payments under its own privacy policy. ExitBound receives verified transaction information, such as product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates and revocation status, to deliver purchases and calculate the annual pass’s expiration. The app does not receive your payment-card details. Restoring the annual pass uses Apple’s purchase history for your Apple Account. Consumable balances remain device-local.

Nearby duels

When you host or join a duel, the app shares a randomly generated player identifier, room identifier, puzzle configuration, readiness, moves, timing and results with the other device. Nearby room discovery advertises room and game-mode information to nearby players. Connected gameplay uses encrypted peer-to-peer sessions. This information is used to connect players and run the match, not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Camera access

If you choose to scan a duel QR code, the app requests camera permission. QR scanning takes place on your device. The app does not save or upload camera images or video. You can deny camera permission and use nearby joining instead. Camera and Local Network permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and investigate the issue. Email is processed by the email providers involved in delivery. We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed to handle the request and related issues or meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

This website

This static website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link subjects your visit to that service’s policies.

Your choices

You can play single-player puzzles offline, decline optional permissions, and avoid optional purchases or duels. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device, but Apple purchase records and device backups are controlled separately by Apple and your settings. Deletion may permanently remove progress and unused consumable balances. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Children

The app has no account-registration form and does not ask players for their age or contact information. If a child sends personal information through support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app’s practices change and will revise the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact phoenixremote.support@gmail.com.

Apple’s services are covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
