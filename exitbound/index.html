Support for ExitBound on iPhone.

Contact support

For help with a purchase, a puzzle or a technical issue, email phoenixremote.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that caused the problem. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or Apple verification codes.

Email support

How do lives recharge?

You can hold up to three lives. Each lost life returns 30 minutes after it was lost, even while the app is closed. The optional one-year unlimited-lives pass removes this wait; it does not renew automatically.

How do hints and extra moves work?

Each attempt includes one free hint reveal. Tap Hint again to apply that hint. Purchased hint packs add 20 reveals. A move pack can be applied to one attempt from the Out of Moves panel. Extra moves do not improve the star rating.

Restore an unlimited-lives pass

Use the same Apple Account that purchased the pass. Open the in-game Store and select Restore unlimited-lives pass. The pass lasts one year from purchase. Hint and move balances are stored on your device and are not restored by this button.

A purchase did not appear

Check your internet connection and reopen ExitBound. A pending purchase is credited after Apple confirms it. If the issue continues, contact support with the product name and approximate purchase date. For refund requests, visit Apple’s Report a Problem website.

Play a nearby duel

Both players need ExitBound and nearby networking enabled. Allow Local Network access when prompted. One player hosts and the other joins nearby or scans the host’s QR code. Camera permission is needed only for QR scanning; you can join nearby without it.

Keep your progress safe

Progress and unused consumable credits are saved locally. ExitBound does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Deleting the app can remove these records, so contact support before reinstalling to troubleshoot.
